# Confirmatory Construct Boundary

| Metric | Measures | Does NOT establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SER | Selective change in the reported canonical primary diagnosis under targeted versus matched-neutral intervention | Hidden chain-of-thought use, causal internal reasoning, or correctness |
| Root correctness | Exact canonical localization at the gold-declared entity level | Complete RCA explanation quality or service-equivalent correctness |
| Fault correctness | Frozen taxonomy-label identification with pre-frozen synonyms | Semantic mechanism depth |
| Evidence Citation Shift | Change in explicit valid evidence-ID references | Complete latent evidence use; absence of citation is not absence of use |
| Confidence delta | Change in self-reported confidence | Calibration or probability of correctness |
| Judge outcomes | Blinded semantic quality of mechanism, causal explanation, grounding, and unsupported claims | Deterministic entity, JSON, evidence-ID, or taxonomy validity |
